## First Golden Forgery patch!
Thank you for playing our mod! We didn't expect so many people to try it, it means a lot to us.

Today we have a hotfix patch aiming to fix some of the bigger issues that were reported, including typos and errors in the command list, as well as some very light changes to a handful of characters.

Head to the [**Install**](/install/) page for the download to the patch. Notes below.

## Patch notes
>*
v0.5.1 Hotfix patch

System fixes:
- Command list typos/errors were fixed up

Battler:
- Fixed a bug with Metasuper laser not disappearing when Battler would get hit
- Moved the laser upwards slightly to make it combo more reliably from point blank in the corner

Beatrice:
- 2AB Stomp hitstun bug on Black Battler fixed

Black Battler:
- 623C fireball now hits in any hitstun state

Dlanor:
- Fixed a bug that would cause the game to softlock when defeating the opponent with Metasuper
- Fixed an issue with Dlanor's sword disppearing early during Metasuper
- Initially j.2C was not supposed to make it, but due to people liking it, we have balanced it and fixed it up.

Erika:
- Removed old scythe moves that were not intended for release

George:
- 236A hitstun increased, fixed bug that would sometimes leave the opponent stuck in the air after parrying
- Fixed an issue with George missing the ground slide hitstun animation

Lambdadelta:
- Fixed an issue with 214A disappearing when going off screen

Lucifer:
- Increased startup of teleport from 3f -> 12f
- Changed the Input of grounded stake projectile to 214X. Air version remains 624.
- Normal grounded stake series now launch on hit

Willard:
- Added 3f extra recovery on 214C feint to prevent an infinite
- Slightly increased hitbox size of 214A
*
