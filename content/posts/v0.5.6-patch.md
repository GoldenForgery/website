## October update!

It's been a few months since the last update, and we have been hard at work behind the scene for a larger update that will be release sometime in the future. With that said, today we have a patch aiming to rework how Beatrice's tower works, as well as some other fun additions, adjustments and bug fixes!

As always, head to the [**Install**](/install/) page to download the patch. Enjoy!
  
## Patch notes

v0.5.6

**Battler**:

- Increased dash length and recovery of 214A (now -6 on block)
- Increased recovery of 6C (now -7 on block)
- Decreased hitbox size of the j.214 series to make it less disjointed

**Beatrice**:

- Towers were reworked in several ways:
  * They no longer go away if you activate meta
  * They no longer have unique Meta versions
  * There is no longer a homing shot, the tower shoots in the direction it faces
  * 22B summons a third tower at the player's position which shoots a delayed shop
- Decreased the startup of Tower Summon

- 214B can now be cancelled into hover
- 2C now has a second hitbox and is a two hit attack

**Black Battler**:

- Made adjustments to make 623SP more reliable as a reversal

**Dlanor**:

- Adjusted the visual effect of 623A to look more like a dash with no hitbox

**Eva-Beatrice**:

- Adjusted the visual effects on 214B/214[C]/214C rekka to better match the animations
- Fixed an issue with Jelly having a hurtbox before it lands on the floor

**George**:

- Fixed an issue with j.236B/C being unblockable at max range
- Adjusted the normal cancel window of c.5C so that you can't cancel as late during the gap of the two hits
- Adjusted the special cancel window c.5C so that you can't special cancel during the gap of the two hits
- Removed hard knockdown on j.2C
- Reduced the super freeze of j.236SP, reduced whiff hitbox and made less disjointed
- Increased landing recovery of j.236SP on whiff
- Decreased horizontal angle of j.236SP
- Reduced j.236SP blockstun

**Kanon**:

- Adjusted the visual effects on the 236X series to make it look better

**Lambdadelta**:

- j.2SP now has a hitbox upon landing
- 3C hurtbox size increased on Lambda's head and back
- j.2B damage reduced to fit other B moves
- 22SP recovery increased
- 236B can now be blocked in the air

**Lucifer**:

- Decreased hitbox size of the j.214 series to make it less disjointed

**Ronove**:

- Fixed an issue causing guardkick to sometimes send the opponent flying behind him

**Shannon**:

- Adjusted 214A/B/C series so that Shannon can only have one active at a time

**Virgilia**:

- Added 6B, which is a huge mirror pushing towards Virgilia. High startup but combos into 2B or C moves, and hitbox duration is is quite long.
- Replaced 3C with a long range dagger attack coming out of the ground
- Added j.214X
  * j.214A/B/C are all the same, Virgilia flies forwards on her broom. Does a hard knockdown and also flies through the opponent
  * j.214SP does not fly through the opponent but does decent damage and multi hit. Also a hard knockdown.
  * Both versions can have their falling animation cancelled into hover or a normal
